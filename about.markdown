I’m an embedded systems and embedded Linux engineer with a background spanning firmware, connected devices, production systems, and low-level software.

This site is a technical notebook, professional blog, and living coursework environment. I use it to develop deeper systems knowledge, document experiments, and turn ongoing study into durable technical material.

Current areas of focus include:

- **NIC firmware and datacenter infrastructure** — packet paths, PCIe, DMA, descriptor rings, interrupts, Linux drivers, RDMA, SmartNICs, and related systems topics.
- **Performance and computer architecture** — caches, memory hierarchies, NUMA, concurrency, latency, throughput, and low-level optimization.
- **OpenBMC and platform firmware** — embedded Linux, boot flows, hardware management, security, telemetry, and fleet-scale reliability.
- **Embedded and IoT security** — secure boot, device identity, cryptography, update systems, trust boundaries, and hardening deployed devices.
- **Trading systems** — market data, exchange architecture, low-latency software, simulation, and quantitative systems engineering.
- **Embodied AI** — research interests around grounded intelligence, active perception, lifelong learning, cortical-inspired representations, and systems that learn through interaction with the physical world.

The coursework here is intentionally iterative. As I study, build labs, debug systems, and refine mental models, the material should evolve with that work rather than remain a static set of notes.
