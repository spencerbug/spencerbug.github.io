## Outcome

The course pairs its evolving technical text with focused interviews from engineers who have built or operated real infrastructure systems.

## Deliverables

- Define interview themes and target practitioner profiles by track.
- Create a question template centered on incidents, constraints, design decisions, and lessons learned.
- Define consent, attribution, editing, transcript, and publication workflow.
- Connect each interview to relevant lessons without treating anecdotes as universal architecture.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Questions elicit practical detail rather than product promotion or generic career advice.
- [ ] Interviewees can review quotations and sensitive details before publication.
- [ ] Vendor-specific practices are labeled with time and context.
- [ ] Each published interview stands on its own while linking back to the course.
