## Outcome

Readers and contributors can tell how material moves from generated draft to studied, tested, reviewed, and maintained course content.

## Why it matters

The value of the course is not that a model can produce a large volume of prose. It is the repeated loop of technical study, learner annotations, primary-source checks, experiments, and expert criticism that turns drafts into durable explanations.

## Deliverables

- Define visible maturity states for course material.
- Specify primary-source and citation expectations.
- Document the browser-annotation review loop and expert handoff format.
- Define how corrections, disputes, obsolete material, and vendor-specific claims are handled.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A reader can distinguish generated draft, author-reviewed, lab-verified, and expert-reviewed material.
- [ ] Review credit and remaining uncertainty can be represented without implying endorsement of the whole course.
- [ ] Technical claims with meaningful change risk have a maintenance path.
- [ ] The policy is short enough to be followed consistently.
